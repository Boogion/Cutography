Cutography

Turn a photo or any place on Earth into stacked layers you can cut, lay out or print. Works with Cricut®, laser cutters, KLayout, and any tool that reads SVG, PNG, GDS, BMP, or TIFF.

You opened this page straight from the folder. The photo tool works, but example photos and the map tool need the app running: double-click Cutography (or start.bat / start.command), then use the page it opens.

Start from a photo

Pick your paper colors, load a picture, and choose how many sheets. The app finds the color regions, cleans up pieces too small to cut, and stacks them.

Open the photo tool →

Start from a map

Paste coordinates from Google Maps and choose how wide an area to show. The app downloads real elevation, streets and water, and turns the terrain into one sheet per contour.

Open the map tool →

What you get

CricutOne SVG with every layer for Design Space, plus per-layer SVGs or PNGs and a Print Then Cut image.
KLayoutA GDSII layout at true size, one layer number per sheet, with a layer file that names each sheet and shows its paper color.
Grayscale EBLNon-overlapping dose levels as GDS (datatype or layer per level, keyhole or hole-free) or a grayscale BMP at your pixel size, plus a dose table and the recipe, for BEAMER.
Structural colorRegions filled with gratings or pillar arrays checked against your process limits, region masks to fill yourself, and a table of every structure.
High-res imagesBlack-and-white layer masks, a color composite and a label map at up to 4800 dpi, as PNG, TIFF or BMP, with a scale file.
